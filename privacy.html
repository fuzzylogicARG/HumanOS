Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Human Operating System handles basic information related to visitors, customers, and digital product delivery.

1. Information We May Collect

We may receive information you provide directly, such as your email address, payment confirmation details, or support messages. Payment details are processed by third-party payment providers and are not stored by us.

2. Payment Providers

Payments may be processed by services such as Stripe or PayPal. These providers may collect and process payment information according to their own privacy policies and security standards.

3. How We Use Information

  • To confirm purchases and deliver digital products.
  • To provide customer support.
  • To respond to questions or access issues.
  • To improve our digital products and customer experience.

4. Data Sharing

We do not sell personal information. We may share limited information only when necessary to process payments, deliver products, provide support, comply with legal obligations, or protect our services.

5. Security

We use reasonable measures to protect information we receive. However, no online transmission or storage method is guaranteed to be completely secure.

6. Digital Product Delivery

Digital products may be delivered manually by email or through secure digital links after payment confirmation. Please keep your purchase confirmation for support purposes.

7. Contact

For privacy questions or support, contact: humanosystem@proton.me
